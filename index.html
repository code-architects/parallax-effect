Depth, without the weight

Three layers, three speeds. Background drifts slower than foreground as you scroll.

How it works

Each background layer tracks the scene's position in the viewport and shifts by position × speed. Slower speed reads as farther away. One scroll listener, rAF-throttled, transform only.

Ship it as-is

Drop it into any hero or section break. Respects reduced motion, keyboard-scrollable, and costs one small script.
